A SMALL MODEL, AN EXPLAINABLE WORKFLOW

From review to category.

Text Atlas classifies English product-related titles and reviews into the existing three-level taxonomy. It does not generate new categories or understand every domain.

01 / Prepare

Normalize Unicode, case and whitespace. Combine title and text identically in training and serving.

02 / Represent

TF-IDF turns words and word pairs into sparse numeric features, emphasizing useful terms.

03 / Classify

Compare a joint-path baseline with level classifiers that score only valid training paths.

Why a hierarchy?

A broad department contains categories, which contain narrower subcategories. Selecting complete paths prevents incompatible parent–child predictions.

Why not a large language model?

This task has a fixed taxonomy and a modest dataset. Lightweight classifiers are inexpensive to run, reproducible and a useful baseline.

Use with judgment

Ranking scores are not calibrated probabilities. Short text, unsupported vocabulary, rare categories and the dataset’s “unknown” labels require human review.

Inputs are processed in memory and are not stored by this application. Hosting infrastructure may record operational request metadata. Avoid submitting confidential or personal information.

Original data provenance and redistribution permission are not established by this demo; see the repository’s data documentation.
